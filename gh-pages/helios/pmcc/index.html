RULES

Executable specification

LongFrom actual AAPL calls 270–450 DTE, test previous-close Delta targets 0.70, 0.75, 0.80, 0.85 and 0.90 (±0.03). Fill at the 10:00 ET ask.
ShortTest weekly-call Delta targets 0.20, 0.25 and 0.30, selecting the nearest prior-close Delta subject to a ±0.10 guardrail. A next-morning price gap does not retroactively remove a valid prior-close candidate. Fill at bid.
CalendarTrade on the first exchange session of the week. A Monday holiday moves entry to the next open session.
ExitBuy back the short at its expiry-session 15:00 ET ask; close the long at the final 15:00 ET bid.
Costs$0.65 per contract per side. Open positions use liquidation value: long bid minus short ask.
No look-aheadThe Delta used to choose a contract is dated and shown for the session before execution.
